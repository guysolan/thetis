## A. Product

### 1. Problem

Achilles rupture patients, whether treated surgically or not, must sleep in a boot for 8 to 12 weeks following injury. More than 3 in 4 struggle to sleep in a boot; ~25% take it off completely. That puts patients at risk of re-rupture.

The Thetis night splint is the only purpose-built **plantarflexion** night splint for Achilles tendon rupture. Surgeons support use for the 8–12 week recovery period **alongside** a walking boot (day boot / night Thetis).

SP AIR WALKER / PLUS with heel wedge already covers daytime ATR immobilisation. The gap is night/rest/hygiene - when patients remove the walker, or are given a reimbursed plantar fasciitis night splint that holds the foot in **dorsiflexion** (wrong direction for ATR).

**Added value:** ~30° plantarflexion at night; ~200 g (~90% lighter than a typical boot); open/breathable; complements SP AIR WALKER rather than replacing it.

*Sleep figures: Thetis survey n=195 - 77% struggled in a boot; 26% removed it; 35% removed or adjusted it.*

### 2. Product samples

**Provided.** Samples, packaging, IFU and kit accessories supplied for SPORLASTIC evaluation. Brochures/flyers available under NDA.

### 3. Competitors

No direct competitor (no other ATR plantarflexion night splint). Adjacent options:

- **Walker + wedges** (SP AIR WALKER PLUS, Aircast, etc.) - day immobilisation in equinus. Right for day, wrong for sleep; complementary, not competing.
- **VACOped** - competing day ATR boot. Day pathway only; not a night solution.
- **PF night splints** (DARCO, PROCARE, UFO; DE codes e.g. 23.03.01.0007 / 0009 / 0019 / 0023) - dorsiflexion stretch; often reimbursed under PG 23. Wrong angle for ATR; still bulky.

All three leave night unprotected or incorrectly protected. Commercial logic for SPORLASTIC: attach Thetis to the existing SP AIR WALKER ATR pathway and pursue a correct Hilfsmittel listing.

### 4. State of development

**TRL 9** - on market since ~2021/22 in multiple countries.

**Done:** CE/UKCA Class I; multi-market registrations; NHS acute pathway use; 5,000+ units in field; UK + US designs granted; US utility allowed/granted (issue fee payment outstanding - not yet on public USPTO/website); EP utility still in examination.

**Next (buyer-led):** in-house manufacture under SPORLASTIC QMS; soft-goods/shell refinement; German Hilfsmittel listing; SP AIR WALKER day + Thetis night bundle.

## B. Clinical benefit and product safety

### 5. Clinical evidence

**Pathway, safety and patient-experience** data - not a large randomised efficacy trial vs boot for re-rupture. Still meaningful for a Class I night orthosis.

- **Royal Surrey QIP (EFORT 2023)** - faster acute pathway with splint vs prior plaster route (e.g. mean time to definitive plan 3.7 vs 8.8 days; A&E→specialist 2.9 vs 8.7 days).
- **Sleeping safe and sound (BOFAS 2024)** - no signal of worse complications/function vs control in presented analysis; authors report 95% CI for night injury risk &lt;0.1%.
- **Mother Knows Best (BOFAS 2023)** - high satisfaction; no adverse outcomes reported vs traditional night boot wear in that series.
- **Sleep survey (n=195)** - quantifies the boot-at-night problem (77% struggle; 26% remove).
- **Field use / PMS** - 5,000+ devices in use; **no formal vigilance complaints or reported re-ruptures attributed to the device** in our PMS framing to date.

**Documents:** conference/QIP PDFs + Technical Documentation 2.1.0 (incl. preclinical sleep testing n=71 uninjured users during development). Public summary and PDF links: [thetismedical.com/evidence](https://thetismedical.com/evidence) - see **Annex A**.

**Ongoing/planned:** no pivotal RCT running; continuous PMS. Further studies open with SPORLASTIC clinical partners.

### 6. Proof of efficacy - what we can and cannot claim

**Supported by the evidence we have**

- Maintains plantarflexion for night/rest use as designed (device principle + usability/preclinical wear testing; clinical pathway adoption).
- Acceptable night-time safety profile in presented BOFAS analysis and PMS to date (no formal device complaints; no attributed re-rupture signal in our tracking).
- Improves the *recovery experience* vs sleeping in a boot (Mother Knows Best; review themes - sleep, comfort, hygiene).
- Useful in acute NHS pathways as an alternative to plaster while awaiting definitive boot/plan (Royal Surrey QIP).

**Reliable marketing claims:** purpose-built ATR plantarflexion night splint; use alongside walking boot for ~8–12 weeks; ~200 g; NHS pathway use; conference-presented safety/experience data; PMS with no formal vigilance complaints to date.

**We do not claim:** proven reduction in re-rupture rate vs boot in an RCT; guaranteed sleep improvement for every patient; treatment of tendinopathy or plantar fasciitis.

## C. Regulation, quality and reimbursement

### 7. Target patient and demarcation

**Intended:** complete ATR (surgical or non-operative); typically within ~12 weeks; night/rest/hygiene alongside daytime walker (e.g. SP AIR WALKER PLUS). Per IFU/tech file: night-time plantarflexion for the first ~3 months; generally after first ~2 weeks or as specialist instructs; do not stand/walk in the splint.

**Not for:** tendinitis/tendinopathy alone; plantar fasciitis; weight-bearing boot replacement.

### 8. Application and adherence

Night / rest / hygiene for the same episode as daytime boot care - not a long-term device (§7).

**No formal adherence RCT and no published discontinuation rate** for the Thetis splint. Boot-at-night non-compliance is the problem it targets (§1 / §5). Continued use is inferred from sales, pathway adoption, and sleep-vs-boot preference (§5, §19, Annexes A–B), not a measured wear-night protocol.

**Dependencies:** RP (shells) and MPD (soft goods/assembly) are the two critical partners; print houses are replaceable. SPORLASTIC may keep them, dual-source, or bring moulding/textiles in-house - intros and specs included either way.

**Handover:** Guy Solan available through redesign, tooling, first production and launch.
