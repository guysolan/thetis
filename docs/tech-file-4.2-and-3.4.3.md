## 4.2 List of applied standards and common specifications

The following standards and specifications are applied (in whole or in relevant part) to demonstrate conformity with the General Safety and Performance Requirements of Regulation (EU) 2017/745 for this Class I, non-invasive, non-sterile, non-measuring device. Where a standard is applied only in part, the applicable clauses are those relevant to intact-skin orthoses, labelling/IFU, risk management, biological evaluation strategy, and quality/traceability processes as implemented by Thetis Medical Ltd.

| Standard / specification | Issue | Application |
| -------------------------- | ------- | ------------- |
| **EN ISO 14971** | 2019 (+ A11:2021 where applicable) | Risk management process throughout the device lifecycle (see §5) |
| **EN ISO 10993-1** | 2018 | Biological evaluation framework — surface device, intact skin, prolonged contact; risk-based testing strategy (see §6.1 / BER-ARS-001) |
| **EN ISO 10993-18** | 2020 | Chemical / material characterisation of patient-contacting materials (see §6.1 / BER-ARS-001) |
| **EN ISO 15223-1** | 2021 | Symbols used on labelling / packaging / IFU information supplied by the manufacturer (see §2.1) |
| **EN ISO 20417** | 2021 | Information to be supplied by the manufacturer (labelling and IFU principles) (see §2) |
| **EN ISO 13485** | 2016 | **Not certified.** Selected QMS practices aligned with MDR Article 10 proportionate to Class I (complaint handling, incident reporting, recall, distribution records, document control). No claim of full EN ISO 13485 certification. |
| **EN ISO 14155** | 2020 | **Not applied** — no MDR Article 62 clinical investigation performed (see §6.7.3) |
| **Common Specifications (MDR Art. 9)** | — | **None applicable** to this device type at the date of this document |

**Standards explicitly not applied (with rationale):**

| Standard | Rationale |
| ---------- | ----------- |
| EN ISO 10993-5 / -10 / -23 | Waived per BER-ARS-001 risk-based biological evaluation (§6.1.3) |
| EN 60601 series (electrical safety / EMC) | Not an active / electrical device |
| Sterilisation / packaging sterile barrier standards (e.g. EN ISO 11607) | Non-sterile device |
| Software lifecycle standards (e.g. EN 62304) | No software |

## 3.4.3 Quality assurance agreements with subcontractors for outsourced production steps

### Policy

Thetis Medical Ltd remains the legal manufacturer and retains overall responsibility for the finished device. Outsourced production steps are controlled by a combination of:

1. Documented purchase specifications / drawings / BOM  
2. Supplier qualification (capability, history, certificates where available)  
3. Incoming inspection of critical parts  
4. Written quality / supply agreements or equivalent purchase terms with critical subcontractors  

Sterilisation and sterile packaging are **not applicable** (non-sterile device).

### Critical subcontractors and control measures

| Process | Subcontractor | QA / supply control in place |
| --------- | --------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Injection moulding (PP shell) | **RP Technologies Limited**, 54 Second Avenue, Pensnett Trading Estate, Kingswinford, West Midlands, DY6 7XJ, United Kingdom | Purchase orders against approved CAD/drawing and material specification (medical polypropylene). Supplier operates under ISO 9001. Incoming inspection of moulded shells (dimensions, finish, L/R identification, flash/defects). Non-conforming product returned or quarantined. |
| Soft goods / fasteners / textile components & related manufacturing | **MPD** (hook-and-loop / Velcro® and agreed textile fastening components) | Purchase against specification (type, width, OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 Class II or equivalent evidence on file). Incoming check for correct component and obvious defects before sewing/assembly. |
| Sewing / assembly of padding and straps to shell | **[NAME — Vgtec Limited or MPD — pick one and use consistently]** | Work instructions / approved sample; assembly to approved BOM. Incoming/final visual inspection of stitch integrity, strap attachment, foam bond/sew quality, left/right and size marking. |
| Primary packaging (printed carton) | Packhelp (or current carton supplier) | Artwork approval by Thetis Medical Ltd; print proof sign-off; spot check of UDI/label content on receipt. |
| IFU printing | Hello Print (or current IFU printer) | Controlled IFU master file; print proof approval; version control on IFU revisions. |

### Quality assurance agreement — minimum content (critical suppliers)

For **RP Technologies** and the sewing/assembly supplier, Thetis Medical Ltd maintains (or will maintain prior to next production run) a written quality / supply agreement or signed purchase specification covering at least:

- Scope of work and approved drawings / revisions  
- Material grade / equivalent approvals (no silent substitution)  
- Traceability (batch / lot identification of shells and critical components)  
- Notification of process, material or site changes before implementation  
- Non-conformance, concession and corrective action process  
- Right to request certificates of conformity / material TDS for the supplied lot  
- Retention of relevant manufacturing records for a period compatible with MDR record retention (minimum 10 years from last placing on the market of the device batch)  
- Confidentiality and restriction on use of Thetis tooling/drawings  

Where a formal dual-signature “Quality Assurance Agreement” PDF is not yet executed, equivalent control is exercised through **approved purchase orders + drawings + incoming inspection records**. Formal QAA documents are filed in the supplier qualification folder and referenced here when signed.

### Statement

No sterilisation or sterile packaging is outsourced. No adhesives, solvents or chemical bonding agents are used in assembly (see §6.1). Changes to critical subcontractors require update of this section, the risk file, and (where relevant) biological evaluation inputs.
