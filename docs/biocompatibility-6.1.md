## 6.1.2 Literature research / component characterisation and evidence

### Risk-based safety summary

The Achilles Tendon Rupture Night Splint is a surface device contacting intact skin (prolonged duration). Biological safety is established via **material characterisation (ISO 10993-18)** and comparison to the **state of the art** for orthopaedic textile/plastic devices with intact-skin contact, bypassing the need for invasive in vivo testing per ISO 10993-1:2018 (Section 6.2.1), where justified by risk and existing evidence.

### Component characterisation and evidence

All materials are sourced from regulated UK/EU suppliers with a documented history of safe medical or technical use on intact skin.

| Component | Supplier | Safety evidence |
| ----------- | ---------- | ----------------- |
| Padding | Bowmer Bond | Medical-grade / REACH compliant |
| Nylon webbing | M. Wright & Sons | MoD/NATO specification (high biocompatibility history) |
| Fasteners / thread (hook-and-loop) | MPD (Velcro®) | OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 (Class II) |
| Elastic straps | Stretchline | OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 (Class II) |
| Rigid shell | RP Technologies | Medical polypropylene (REACH / TDS) |

Body-contact materials in finished form: EVA foam (padding), elastic, nylon, silicone (strap overlays where used), polypropylene (shell).

### Manufacturing declaration (inert assembly)

- **Process:** Mechanical assembly via industrial sewing only.  
- **Exclusions:** No adhesives, solvents, bonding agents, or post-assembly chemical treatments are used.  
- **Conclusion:** The biological profile of the finished device remains identical to the characterised raw materials. No manufacturing-induced toxicological risks (ISO 10993-1) are introduced by assembly.

## 6.1.3 Test reports of performed biological tests

No ISO 10993-5 (cytotoxicity), ISO 10993-10 (irritation / sensitisation), or ISO 10993-23 testing was performed on the finished device.

### Justification for waiving ISO 10993-5 / 10 / 23 testing

1. **Cytotoxicity — waived.** Materials are non-leaching technical / medical polymers and textiles for intact-skin contact. OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 (Class II) certification on relevant textile components supports absence of harmful levels of heavy metals, VOCs and formaldehyde as screened under that scheme. Polypropylene shell supported by REACH / TDS from the moulding supplier.  
2. **Irritation / sensitisation — waived.** The material stack (polypropylene shell, EVA padding, nylon/elastic straps with hook-and-loop fasteners, silicone overlays where used) is consistent with the **state of the art** for orthopaedic braces, walking-boot liners/straps and ankle-foot orthoses with a long history of safe intact-skin contact. Mechanical irritation from overtightening is controlled by design and IFU precautions (loosen straps if tingling or pain occurs).

Full rationale: annex **BER-ARS-001**.
