CLAIMS

1. An orthopaedic device (1), the device comprising a splint (2) and attachment means (4,
5, 6) for removably securing the splint to a user’s foot and lower leg, wherein the splint
(2) is configured such that in use, the user’s foot and leg are maintained at an obtuse
angle to one another and relative movement between the foot and the leg is substantially
prevented, wherein the splint (2) comprises a leg section (2a) and a foot section (2b), the
leg section defining a first plane, the foot section defining a second plane, characterised
in that the sections are rigidly joined such that the angle between the planes is from about
95° to about 155°, wherein the leg section (2a) and the foot section (2b) are sections of
a single splint body, each said section including a dorsal side (21) and a ventral side
(22), the leg section ventral side being substantially conformed to the shape of a part of
the leg of a patient and the foot section ventral side being substantially conformed to the
shape of a part of a foot of a patient, wherein the leg section ventral side is conformed
to the shape of a shin of a patient and the foot section ventral side is conformed to the
shape of the dorsal side of the foot of a patient, wherein the leg section ventral side and
the foot section ventral side are cross-sectionally curved, further comprising a rigid shell
which matches the anatomical contours of the shin, ankle and top of the foot, wherein
the attachment means comprises a plurality of straps (4, 5, 6) disposed to releasably
secure the splint to the user, wherein the attachment means comprises an ankle strap (5).

2. A device according to any preceding claim, wherein the splint includes padding, wherein
the padding is disposed on the ventral side of the leg section and/or the ventral side of
the foot section of the splint.
3. A device according to any preceding claim, wherein the splint comprises a substantially
rigid plastics material.
4. A device according to claim 1, further comprising a calf strap (6) and a foot strap,
wherein the straps are fixed to be perpendicular to the edge of the rigid shell, between
said shell and said padding.

5. A device according to any one of claims 1 to 4, wherein the straps comprise securing
means such that they may affix to themselves once attached to the user.
6. A device according to any one of claims 4 to 5, wherein the ankle strap comprises an
aperture, wherein the aperture is provided in the form of a slit (7).
7. A device according to any one of claims 4 to 6 wherein a heel cup is provided on the
ankle strap.
8. A device according to claim 6 or 7, wherein the slit and/or heel cup (12) enables the
strap to wrap around the heel of the user.
9. A device according to any preceding claim, wherein the device comprises different
splints for the user’s left and right leg, and/or different sizes of splint in order to match
the anatomy of the user.
10. A device according to any preceding claim, wherein the device is adapted to be attached
to the user by a bandage, sock (13), or sleeve, wherein the device further comprises a
pouch.
