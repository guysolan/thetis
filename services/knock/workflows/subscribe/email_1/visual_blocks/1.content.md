Recovery from an Achilles rupture is tricky. We work with surgeons and physios to create resources that help patients get back to work and sport as safely as possible.

Over the coming weeks, we'll send a handful of short, practical emails covering early protection, talking to your specialist, and recovering safely. No spam — just the essentials.

For now, there is just one rule: keep your foot in a tiptoe position (pointed down). This pushes the ends of the tendon together and allows them to heal.
